Privacy

Sara777 - Privacy Policy

1. Our Philosophy: Privacy as a Pillar of Integrity

At Sara777, we believe that your privacy is the foundation of your success. In a digital world where personal information is often tracked and traded, Sara777 stands as a secure place for your research. We do not just host data; we protect the people who use it.

By using Sara777, you are entering a secure environment. Our systems are global, using advanced technology to ensure high-speed performance. This means your data may be handled on secure servers located outside of your home country. This document explains our commitment to keeping your data private and safe from third parties.

2. Executive Privacy Summary | संक्षिप्त सारांश

For the modern analyst who values time, here is our “Zero-Compromise” summary:

English:

  • Zero Spam Architecture: We have a strict policy against the sale of phone numbers or personal details. Your data never reaches a telemarketer.
  • Encrypted Sovereignty: All user interactions are shielded by high-level encryption on secure, international-grade servers.
  • Absolute Control: Users maintain the “Right to be Forgotten.” Data erasure can be triggered instantly via our verified Sara777 Telegram Bot i.e. @sara777_supportbot
  • Maturity Mandate: Access is strictly reserved for individuals aged 18 and above.

हिंदी (Hindi):

  • जीरो स्पैम पॉलिसी: हम आपके फोन नंबर या व्यक्तिगत डेटा को कभी किसी तीसरे पक्ष को नहीं बेचते।
  • एन्क्रिप्टेड सुरक्षा: आपकी सभी जानकारी अंतरराष्ट्रीय स्तर के सुरक्षित सर्वरों पर AES-256 एन्क्रिप्शन के साथ सुरक्षित है।
  • पूर्ण नियंत्रण: आपको अपना डेटा मिटाने का पूरा अधिकार है। आप हमारे टेलीग्राम बॉट के माध्यम से तुरंत अपना डेटा डिलीट कर सकते हैं।
  • केवल 18+ आयु: यह प्लेटफॉर्म केवल 18 वर्ष और उससे अधिक आयु के उपयोगकर्ताओं के लिए है।

3. Cross-Border Data Architecture & Security

To maintain fast results, Sara777 uses a global network of data centers. Discover the story, mission, and vision of Sara777 on our About Us page.

  • International Transfers: When you access a chart, your technical data may cross international borders. We manage these transfers with strict contracts to ensure your information is well-protected.
  • The Sara777 Vault: We use strong encryption, similar to what banks use, to protect our databases. Whether your data is saved on our servers or moving to your device, it remains unreadable to anyone who should not see it.
  • Voluntary Consent: Providing data at Sara777 is 100% voluntary. You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. Please note that withdrawing consent may stop some features from working correctly.

4. The “No-Leak” Guarantee: Third-Party Neutrality

Sara777 is a platform that does not sell your data. This is a promise overseen by our Lead Analyst, Charan Sharma.

We do not rent, trade, or sell your personal data to marketing agencies or insurance firms. Our focus is on providing high-quality data, not on exploiting your contact information. Your data stays within the Sara777 system to help improve your experience. Understand the rules governing platform usage by reading our Terms & Conditions.

5. Taxonomy of Data Collection

Under the leadership of Charan Sharma, Sara777 follows a strict rule to only collect the data necessary to provide a great experience.

  • Identity Data: Your mobile number is used only to send a one-time password (OTP) for security, which helps us prevent bots from entering our site.
  • Technical Data: We look at things like IP addresses and device info to help our system load results in under 2 seconds.
  • Analytical Usage Data: By tracking traffic on high-volume archives, our team can keep the site running smoothly during busy hours.
  • Security Data: We monitor for irregular traffic to protect the platform from attacks, keeping Sara777 stable and reliable for everyone.

6. The “Sovereign User” Rights

At Sara777, you own your digital footprint. You have the following rights:

  • Right of Access: You can ask for a summary of the data linked to your account at any time.
  • Right to Rectification: You can update your contact details or preferences in your profile settings.
  • Right to Erasure: You can ask for your account to be deleted. Once you verify your request through our Telegram Bot @sara777_supportbot, your data is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

7. Strict 18+ Age Governance

Sara777 is for mature market analysis. Our tools require responsibility that is reserved for adults. Need help with charts or results? Check our FAQ page for quick answers.

  • Prohibited Access: Anyone under 18 is not allowed to use Sara777.
  • Active Monitoring: If we find that a user is a minor, the account will be closed immediately, and all data will be removed.

8. Functional Cookies vs. Tracking Cookies

We use technology to help you, not to follow you. Learn about important usage limitations in our Disclaimer section.

  • Functional Cookies: These files remember your preferences, like whether you prefer a Night or Day view, so you don’t have to change them every time you visit.
  • No Cross-Site Tracking: We do not use “Advertising Cookies.” We are not interested in your activity on other websites.
  • User Control: You can turn off cookies in your browser, but this might make some real-time features work less smoothly.

9. Legal Basis for Data Processing

Sara777 processes data to provide the reliable service we promise. We must use technical data to keep our environment secure and free from fraud.

We do not use your personal information for automated decision-making or to create profiles about your personal behavior. Our focus remains on the data in our charts.

10. Contact & Grievance Redressal

If you have questions about privacy, we are here to help. For support and inquiries, visit our Contact Us section.

  • Verified Communication: All requests or corrections must be sent through our Telegram portal: @sara777_supportbot
  • Response Time: Our team aims to answer all data-related questions within 48 to 72 hours.
  • Lead Oversight: Privacy audits are conducted every few months by Charan Sharma to keep our system secure.

11. Version Integrity & Updates

We update our security practices as technology changes.

  • Document Version: 3.1 (Sara777 Elite Edition)
  • Current Status: Active as of March 2026
  • Next Scheduled Audit: September 2026

By continuing to use Sara 777, you agree to this Privacy Protocol and can conduct your research with confidence.

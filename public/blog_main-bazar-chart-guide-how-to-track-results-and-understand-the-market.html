Understanding the Main Bazar Chart: What are Panna and Jodi?

To master the Main Bazar chart with DPSara 777, you must understand the two fundamental components that make up every result: the Panna (Panel) and the Jodi. Think of the chart as a structured ledger on DPSara777 where these two elements work together to give you a complete picture of a session.

  • What is a Panna (Panel)? The Panna is the three-digit number you see in the chart. It acts as the "foundation" or the core result of the session.   • What is a Jodi? The Jodi is the two-digit pair that appears alongside the Panna. It is derived from the Open and Close results. While the Panna shows the specific draw, the Jodi is what many players focus on for their daily tracking.

Why this matters: When you look at the main bazar panel chart on DPSara 777, you aren't just looking at random numbers, you are looking at a sequence. Understanding how these two values interact helps you read the market with much more clarity.

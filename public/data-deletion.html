Data Deletion Request – Sara777

At Sara777, we believe you should have complete control over your digital footprint. As a platform committed to transparency and your privacy, we make the process of removing your data straightforward, secure, and permanent.

If you wish to remove your account and all associated information from our servers, you may exercise your “Right to be Forgotten” by following the steps below.

How to Request Data Deletion on Sara777

Sara777 utilize a centralized, secure process to handle deletion requests to ensure that your data is not just hidden, but permanently scrubbed from our systems. Before submitting a deletion request, you may wish to learn more about our platform and privacy-first approach on the About Us page.

The Official Deletion Channel: All data deletion requests must be processed through our verified Telegram support portal of Sara 777: @sara777_supportbot

Steps to Purge Your Data:

  • Open the @sara777_supportbot on Telegram.
  • Type and send the command: /delete_my_data
  • Follow the automated prompts to verify your account ownership.
  • Once verified, your request will be queued for processing.

Use of Sara777 services remains subject to our Terms & Conditions, which outline important account and platform policies.

What Happens After You Submit a Request on Sara 777?

Once your request is successfully submitted and verified at Sara 777, the following steps occur to ensure complete removal:

  • Immediate Deactivation: Your account will be deactivated instantly, preventing any further access or data collection.
  • Hard-Purge Protocol: Within 72 hours, your personal identifiers, contact details, and account metadata will be “Hard-Purged” from our active servers.
  • Residual Log Scrubbing: Our technical team ensures that no “ghost” profiles or residual logs remain. All associated records are scrubbed from our global server nodes within 30 days.
  • Confirmation: Once the process is complete, you will receive a final confirmation notification via the support bot.

Important Considerations Before Deleting Your Data on Sara777

Before you proceed for data deletion on sara 777, it is vital to understand the implications of account deletion. Our infrastructure is designed for precision, and because of our commitment to your privacy, this process is irreversible.

  • Irreversibility: Please be aware that once the data deletion process is completed, it is irreversible. All your research history, preferences, and account settings will be permanently lost and cannot be recovered by our team.
  • Voluntary Consent: Data provision at Sara777 is voluntary. By requesting deletion, you are exercising your sovereign right to remove your information from our analytical ecosystem.
  • Security: We require verification through our official Telegram bot to prevent unauthorized deletion requests. We cannot process deletion requests via email or public channels to protect the security of your account.

For detailed information about how we collect, store, process, and protect user information, please review our Privacy Policy.

Data Integrity During the Transition

One of the most common questions we receive is: “What happens to the global market data if I delete my account?”

It is important to distinguish between Personal Identity Data (your phone number, preferences, and login logs) and Global Analytical Data (market results, historical panel charts, and Jodi records). When you delete your account, only your Personal Identity Data is removed. The global market records remain intact, as they serve the entire community of researchers.

Sara777 protect your privacy while simultaneously protecting the collective history of the market. Your specific usage statistics, such as how many times you accessed a specific page, are anonymized the moment you delete your account, ensuring that your individual behavior can never be traced back to you. Frequently asked questions

Is it really necessary to use the Telegram bot to delete my data?+−

Yes. To protect your privacy and security, we have a strict verification policy. If we allowed account deletion via email or other non-secure channels, malicious actors could potentially delete accounts they do not own. By requiring you to authenticate via our verified Telegram bot @sara777_supportbot, we ensure that the person requesting the deletion is the actual account holder. This adds an essential layer of security to your personal information.

What happens to my research history and personal settings after I delete my account?+−

Once you initiate the deletion process, your entire personalized research environment is permanently removed. This includes any saved market preferences, custom notification settings, and browsing history linked to your account ID. Because we do not store "archived" versions of deleted accounts, this data becomes inaccessible to both you and our technical team. If you plan to continue your research in the future, we recommend noting down any personal analytical findings before requesting deletion.

Does deleting my account impact the global historical charts on the site?+−

Not at all. There is a clear distinction between your personal account data (which is private) and the global market data (which is public information). When you delete your account, you are only purging the data that identifies you as a unique user. The historical panel charts, Jodi records, and starline archives remain on the platform to serve our community of researchers. Your individual browsing habits and account metadata are scrubbed, while the global integrity of our database is maintained.

How long does it truly take for my data to be "completely gone"?+−

While your account is deactivated instantly upon verification of your request, the "Hard-Purge" protocol is a multi-stage process. Your personal identifiers are stripped from our primary servers within 72 hours. Following this, we perform a comprehensive sweep of our backup infrastructure and security logs within 30 days. We provide this 30-day window to ensure that even data trapped in deep-storage backups or distributed server nodes is sanitized, leaving absolutely no trace of your account behind.

Can I rejoin Sara777 after I have deleted my account?+−

Yes, you are always welcome to return. However, because our deletion process is permanent and irreversible, you cannot "reactivate" your old account. If you choose to return to the platform, you will need to register as a new user. Your previous research history, personalized alerts, and account preferences will not be restored, as those records were purged from our systems during your previous deletion request. We recommend this as a fresh start for your analytical journey.

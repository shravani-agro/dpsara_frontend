How to Play Satta Matka – The Basic Structure

Understanding how to play satta matka starts with understanding the basic structure of every satta matka session. Each satta matka session declares three values: an open number, a jodi, and a close number. The open number is declared first — typically at the session's opening time. The jodi is the two-digit number formed by combining the last digit of the open result and the last digit of the close result. The close number is the final value declared at the session's closing time. These three values — open, jodi, and close — form the complete result for every satta matka session. Matka Open Close Numbers – How They Work

The matka open close numbers are the two key result values declared in every satta matka session. The open number is declared at the start of the session and the close number is declared at the end — each is a three-digit number between 100 and 999. The last digit of the open number and the last digit of the close number together form the jodi — the two-digit result that players follow most closely in satta matka research. For example, if the open number ends in 6 and the close number ends in 3, the jodi is 63.
